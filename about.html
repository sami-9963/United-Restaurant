Our Highest Priority

Spotless Cleanliness & Medical-Grade Hygiene

At United Restaurant, hygiene is not just a checklist — it is the cornerstone of our culinary philosophy. We hold ourselves to international 5-star sanitation protocols so that every guest enjoys total peace of mind.

From the moment raw ingredients arrive at our loading docks in United City to the final plate presented at your table, every single step is protected by certified sanitization measures.

  • UV-C Sterilization: All cutlery, glassware, and tableware undergo thermal high-temp washing and UV-C sterilization.
  • Triple RO Purified Water: 100% purified water used across food preparation, sauces, beverages, and crystal ice cubes.
  • Hourly Sanitization Cycles: Dining tables, door handles, and kitchen stations disinfected every 60 minutes.
  • Open Exhibition Kitchen: Full transparency — our glass-fronted kitchen allows guests to observe pristine cleanliness firsthand.

Pristine Food Prep Standards

Dedicated color-coded prep boards for meats, poultry, and vegetables prevent any cross-contamination. Chefs wear sanitized headwear, food-safe silicone gloves, and protective aprons at all times.

Strict Cold-Chain Sourcing

Prime cuts of beef, Norwegian salmon, and farm-fresh chicken are transported under strict temperature-controlled cold chains, preserving freshness, flavor, and microbial safety.

Eco-Clean Sanitization

We utilize organic, food-safe eco disinfectants that eliminate 99.99% of bacteria without leaving chemical odors or residues, ensuring your dining space smells clean, natural, and inviting.
